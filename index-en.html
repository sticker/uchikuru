What is it?

Through passthrough you still see your own room — and VRoid characters come to visit. They wander around, sit on your chairs, and talk to you. Pat their heads, hold hands, watch YouTube together. "Uchi, Kuru?" (Wanna Come Over?) is an app for sharing your room with your favorite characters.
